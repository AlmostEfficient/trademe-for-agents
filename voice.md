# Writing listings

Write like the seller is texting a mate about something they own. Casual, specific, easy to picture. Trade Me buyers skim, so a short listing that answers their questions beats a long one.

## Start from their words

The seller's own description is the best raw material. Keep their phrasing, humour and spelling where it works; tidy only what would confuse a buyer. If they ask for exact wording, use it exactly.

Match their style. If they write in lowercase with no fuss, the listing should too (brand names and model numbers keep their real capitals). If they write formally, so does the listing.

## What goes in

1. What it is, in a line. Brand, model, size, colour.
2. What it's like to own or use, if they said. "super light, comfy for a full day" is worth more than a spec sheet.
3. Condition, in their words. Every fault they mentioned, plainly. A buyer who finds an undisclosed scratch leaves bad feedback.
4. What's included (box, cables, spare parts) and what isn't, if a buyer would expect it.
5. Measurements or specs that help someone decide: clothing measurements, dimensions for furniture, compatibility for tech. Short lines, not a pasted marketing paragraph. A manufacturer link is fine when it helps someone check specs.
6. Pickup and shipping, briefly: "pickup herne bay, or can ship at buyer's cost."

## What stays out

- Anything you made up. No condition, wear, age, history, measurements or "barely used" unless the seller said it or a label shows it. If you're unsure, leave it out or ask.
- Filler: "great item", "don't miss out", "perfect for anyone who…", "excellent condition" (unless those are their words).
- Corporate words: seamless, powerful, elevate, premium quality, must-have.
- Em dashes. Use full stops and commas.
- The purchase price, unless the seller wants it mentioned ("paid $200, selling for $90" can help).
- Personal details beyond the pickup suburb.

## Photos that aren't of the actual item

If a stock or manufacturer photo is added, put it last and say so simply: "last pic is from the Peak Design site."

## Example

Seller said: "alpha direct sweater, wore it once on the milford track, sheds a bit cos it's new, paid like 200 with shipping. length 74 chest 48"

> wore once for a few hours on the milford track. super light, as new condition. will shed a bit since it's still new.
>
> about 190g, packs small.
>
> length 74cm
> chest 48cm
>
> pickup herne bay, or can ship.

(the weight came from the label in the photos. "packs small" was in a later message from the seller.)
